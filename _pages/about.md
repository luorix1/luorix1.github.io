I am a first-year student in the MS in Mechanical Engineering (Research Track) working with Professor Inseung Kang at the [MetaMobility Lab](https://metamobility.cmu.edu/home) at Carnegie Mellon University. My work focuses on readily deployable deep learning–based controllers for exosuits, along with related problems in kinematics and kinetics estimation.

Previously, I worked extensively with IMU-based sensing for gait analysis and with human-in-the-loop exosuit personalization for people with Parkinson’s disease. I am interested in gait analysis and wearable-device control that go beyond the lab, and in how simulation-based studies can inform interventions for pathological gait in clinical populations.
